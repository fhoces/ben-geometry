# Geometry Watch List 🎬📐
*A 4-week sequence to get an 11-year-old hooked on geometry*

> **Note:** Vi Hart's YouTube channel was taken down in 2025, so her videos are linked via Khan Academy (free, ad-free, no account needed).

---

## Week 1: The Hooks
*Short, high wow-per-minute. Pair with the paper craft!*

1. **Vi Hart: Hexaflexagons**
   https://www.khanacademy.org/math/math-for-fun-and-glory/vi-hart/hexaflexagons/v/hexaflexagons
   → *Activity: make one together with a strip of printer paper*

2. **Think Twice: visual proof animations** (browse the channel, ~1 min each)
   https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9yt3wz-6j19RwD5m5f6HSg

3. **Numberphile: The Scientific Way to Cut a Cake**
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBU9N35ZHIw

## The Method

- **Pair each week with the hands-on activity:** the doing cements it more than the watching
- **If he stalls, skip forward:** momentum beats order
- **Watch which direction he leans:** paper-folding/hands-on → more Vi Hart & Matt Parker; animated "why" videos → more 3B1B & Mathologer
